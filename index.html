Reflection

This week, our teacher raised an interesting question in class: Can something created by a computer be considered art? I think the answer is yes.

In my understanding, art is a way of expressing ideas and meanings. How these ideas are expressed and understood can be influenced by the artist's intentions. For example, some abstract paintings may look difficult to understand, but they can still be meaningful if the artist has a clear idea behind them. I think creative coding works in a similar way.

Another important point is originality. Other people may be able to copy the same code and create something that looks similar, but the original idea and thinking process still belong to the creator. For me, the creative thinking behind a work is just as important as the final result.

This week, we also learned about random() and noise(). These functions showed me how randomness can bring more possibilities into digital art. Even when people use the same code, the results may be different each time the program runs. This makes creative coding more interesting because the artist cannot completely control every result.

I also think this unpredictability makes digital artworks more unique. A simple line or shape can develop in unexpected ways through code. This means that even a small program can create many different visual experiences.

Overall, this week helped me understand that creative coding is not just about writing code. It is also about using technology to express personal ideas and explore new possibilities in art.
